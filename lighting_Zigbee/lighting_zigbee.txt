
Lighting automation using Zigbee devices in Home Assistant.

Auxiliary elements, such as timer, input_boolean, python_script, are created in the Home Assistant.

Sensors are selected for a specific space; what is shown here is their actual operation in a given environment.

A sun angle sensor is created using an auxiliary element  ha. (sensor.raschetnyi_ugol_solntsa; device class, measurement angle; unit of measurement: degree)
